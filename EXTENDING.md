# Extending TextData

We will extend TextData with additional textual sources, analytical methods, agent capabilities, and empirical applications. Continued development will address four areas:

- **Research foundation:** extend the literature syntheses, enrich source-linked reading records, update the AI-systems and agent-design resources, and assess additional textual sources.
- **Qualitative inquiry:** extend and evaluate empirical applications, examine alternative interpretations, and develop case selection, within-case and cross-case analysis, and theory development for the Eisenhardt toolkit.
- **Measurement and annotation:** evaluate the proposed measurement designs, obtain independent reference annotations, and assess accuracy and consistency before scaling the annotation workflow.
- **Adaptive infrastructure:** adapt and evaluate the engine for broader use, integrate advances in models and agent methods, and develop further tools and workflows under researcher direction.

TextData is designed for shared development. A new source-acquisition workflow, analytical tool, agent instruction, methodological resource, or empirical application can extend the infrastructure’s capabilities. Our aim is an open-source system through which scholars can develop new ways of investigating text data and make those capabilities available for others to examine, reuse, and extend.
