## Limits

A profile is dated; it is not a promise that the source still looks like this. Recheck the provider's pages before acquisition.

Source-specific limits:

- Accounting-consolidating parenthood is not all beneficial ownership, voting control or everyday decision authority.
- Entities with LEIs are a selected population, not a census of firms.
- Current Golden Copy relationships do not supply a full historical ownership panel; examples in schema documentation are not observed validation cases. Do not assume an automatic CIK–LEI bridge.
