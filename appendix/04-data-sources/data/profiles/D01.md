## Limits

A profile is dated; it is not a promise that the source still looks like this. Recheck the provider's pages before acquisition.

Source-specific limits:

- Ratings and helpful votes are reception measures, not novelty labels or an unbiased quality standard.
- Items are not firms; review selection and product survival can distort organizational inferences.
- Actual files need schema inspection: documentation and examples vary in timestamp/helpful-vote naming and price type. User identifiers and review text need a deliberate sharing plan.
