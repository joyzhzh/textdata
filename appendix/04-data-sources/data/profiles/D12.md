## Unit of analysis

English meeting recordings and synchronized annotations; approximately two-thirds scenario design meetings and one-third natural meetings.

## Coverage and dates

Release: Manual annotations v1.6.2, 2017-04-10 (content unchanged since 2014-06-16); automatic annotations v1.5.1, 2017-04-10.

Time: Time-aligned word-level transcription, speaker turns and meeting sequences. Scenario projects comprise four meetings with four assigned roles.

Size: About 100 hours; manual annotation ZIP 22 MB; automatic annotations 68 MB. Annotation types/coverage vary by session.

## Content: fields and the identifiers that link records

Meeting and speaker IDs, timed words, dialogue acts, topics and summaries; audio/video, slides and other meeting artifacts where available.

## Limits

A profile is dated; it is not a promise that the source still looks like this. Recheck the provider's pages before acquisition.

Source-specific limits:

- There is no AI treatment; scenario meetings are not natural firm decisions.
- Public talk does not reveal unspoken intentions, off-meeting work, actual approval powers or latent learning.
- Existing dialogue-act labels can assist retrieval but do not validate a new organizational construct.

## The review's decision on this source

Decision (2026-10-11): Starter choice for process-rich material with a small annotation package and explicit core licensing.
