## Unit of analysis

Wikipedia talk-page conversational actions and reconstructed discussion relationships across English, German, Russian, Chinese and Greek.

## Coverage and dates

Release: Creator corpus reconstructed from Wikipedia dumps dated 2018-07-01; creator repository read-only/archived 2026-09-04. ConvoKit supplies year/language subsets with their own representations.

Time: Timestamps, revision/action histories and reply relationships support temporal reconstruction. Final utterance text can include later modifications.

Size: Creator English corpus reports 241,288,668 actions and 90,930,244 conversations under its technical definition. ConvoKit's documented English-2003 example has 140,265 utterances and 91,787 conversations; these are not interchangeable counts.

## Content: fields and the identifiers that link records

Action/utterance, speaker, conversation, page/revision and reply/parent identifiers; text, time, modification/restoration metadata. ConvoKit's representation must be distinguished from raw actions.
