# Notice: third-party sources, credits and licences

This component is a review. It contains no third-party data records. It contains the review's own profiles of 25 source families, which quote briefly from and paraphrase the providers' public documentation. Its source evidence cites those pages by address.

## The 25 source families

Every family is third-party material, described and not included. Each profile in `data/catalogue.json` records:

- the provider;
- the entry point the review inspected;
- the reuse status and terms as the provider's pages stated them on 11 October 2026.

Ten pages were rechecked on 29 October 2026 (`data/provider_checks.json`). These statements are the review's record of what the providers said. They are not licences granted by this component, and they can change. To acquire a family, go to its provider and use it under that provider's current terms.
