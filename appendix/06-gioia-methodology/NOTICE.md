## Synthetic example and modifications

The practice text accompanies the skill. An AI assistant wrote the six-line interview passage and analytic memo for the worked example. All speech and test fixtures are synthetic; there are no participant records. The original memo, passages, four requests and saved reports are unchanged. The memo’s unsupported negative-case inference is explained in the [worked example](README.md#worked-example).

This edition uses “researcher” for the human role, clarifies maintenance guidance and quotation offsets, and provides usage instructions, the AI prompt and a file manifest. The package verifier checks the memo fingerprint and replays the retained reports. These changes do not correct or replace the original memo’s inference.
