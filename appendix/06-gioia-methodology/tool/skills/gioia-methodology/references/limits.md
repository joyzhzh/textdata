# Claim and evaluation limits

## Claims need a type and a scope

Distinguish an attributed prescription, an author's report of their practice, a directly inspected source statement, an analyst's interpretation, a tooling proposal and an observed result. Cite the available kind of support. Metadata, abstracts, later recollections and an attractive data structure cannot establish what an unread earlier work did. If a requested historical comparison lacks complete relevant bodies, state the missing cells as unknown and bound any comparison to what was actually inspected.

For a stopping claim record question/construct, unit, procedure, inspected coverage, inaccessible and unsearched routes, positive and adverse tails, and why work stopped. Use a bounded phrase such as `analysis of the supplied excerpts is complete for this request`. Do not turn a cap, convenience sample, silence or no newly found code into saturation. Unknown totals are not zero; a named construct such as saturation needs an explicit definition and evidence in its own scope.

## AI and other performance claims

Attach task, model/version and whether author-labeled or independently established, language, period, dataset/sample, prompt/condition, reference construction, unit, denominator, exclusions and dependence to each claim. A segment-code decision is not a participant; overlap with human themes is not interpretive accuracy. Do not pool unlike tasks, dependent source families or incompatible denominators. Keep favorable, adverse and null tails, and do not transfer an old or unspecified model result to a current product. Human-review benefit needs a specific intervention, comparator and measured outcome; it is not automatic.

## What this package establishes

The instructions and helper implement bounded assistance. The helper can report matching byte spans in explicitly supplied UTF-8 text. It does not verify the authenticity of the source, extraction fidelity to an unseen original, page layout, consent, legal rights, qualitative meaning, privacy, methodological validity or general retrieval performance. User-declared permission fields remain declarations.

The underlying research proposals remain unvalidated on their methodological and engineering merits. Local self-tests are not an independent evaluation, release decision, field-wide membership criterion or claim of improved analysis. This package includes no article corpus, source captures, participant data or literature-search method. For maintenance of supplied records, the skill calls for explicit plans and preservation of earlier versions.

For later evaluation, use separately authorized material and decisions fixed independently of the implementation. Seed missing and ambiguous passages, versions, changed bytes, normalization differences, splices and permission uncertainty. Distinguish literal fidelity from contextual adequacy; measure reviewer effort and erroneous objections as well as detection. Any recall-like result needs benchmark origin, inclusion rules, indexed denominator and exclusions and remains benchmark-relative. A self-authored synthetic case is an engineering check, never an independent reference standard.
