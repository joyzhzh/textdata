# Methodological assistance

## Start with the analytic question

Identify the question, unit (person, interview, episode, segment, decision, etc.), context and permitted excerpt set. Work with the subset already authorized when other material is unavailable. Without excerpts, explain the approach or prepare a blank decision scaffold; do not invent observed codes. Synthetic examples are allowed only when clearly labeled and kept apart from empirical material.

The first-order/second-order/aggregate distinction is an analytic aid, not an obligation to produce three completed columns. The procedures below implement proposed assistance, not a universal Gioia standard or a claim that a diagram confers rigor.

## Work through a small, traceable comparison

1. **Preserve expressions.** Record the supplied expression, speaker or safe pseudonym, manifestation/version and exact span. Keep context about the question, event, negation, uncertainty, translation and relevant surrounding text. A source author's report of a participant is still mediated testimony; it is not newly collected participant data. A paraphrase or translation is labeled and linked to the unchanged original, not put in quotation marks as verbatim speech.
2. **Offer concepts as proposals.** Compare what the excerpts support and where they differ. Suggest a small set of candidate researcher concepts with a rationale and explicit origin (`researcher`, `model`, or attributed author). Preserve any supplied first-order label; distinguish a label constructed by an analyst from words actually used by a participant. Do not seed a universal Gioia codebook.
3. **Challenge the grouping.** Give a plausible competing reading and a concrete negative or complicating case, linked to input if observed. If none was searched or provided, say `not assessed in the supplied set`, not `no negative cases`. Record exclusions and their reasons. If a rival is generated, label it a proposed rival rather than a participant view.
4. **Leave aggregation open.** Propose a second-order theme or aggregate dimension only with a transparent transformation from the lower-level material. Record unresolved membership and scope; do not invent missing categories, discard awkward excerpts or force every row under one dimension.
5. **Separate relationships.** For a proposed process edge, specify endpoints, relation type, unit/time/case, supporting events or comparisons, rival explanation and disconfirming evidence. Co-membership in a category is not sequence; a sequence alone is not causation. With insufficient support, label the edge `hypothesis / warrant missing` and say what evidence would discriminate it.

Use the analytic-decision portion of the [templates](../assets/working-templates.md) only as much as needed. A diagram, when requested, should visibly distinguish proposal nodes and hypothetical edges and have an accompanying evidence/warrant key.

## Record decisions without manufacturing consensus

A suggestion begins `proposed`. Use `adopted`, `rejected`, `revised` or `deferred` only from an explicit researcher decision; preserve who decided, when, why and their actual scope. If the researcher asks for a recommendation, provide one with the rationale but leave adoption pending. A model cannot adopt on the researcher's behalf. Adoption changes decision state, not evidentiary origin or truth.

Preserve the original suggestion text and each successor, including rejection, dissent and unchosen alternatives. Link predecessors and note how the interpretation changed; never retitle a label in a way that erases its earlier meaning. If the task includes interview-guide or sampling revision, retain the prior version, trigger, changed questions/selection and timing. Separate changing respondent composition from within-person change; without linked membership, that distinction remains unresolved.

Record literature consultation when it actually happened: time or explicitly retrospective timing, concept affected, source/version, reason and rival considered. Do not reconstruct a neat chronology after the fact without labeling it retrospective. Existing theory may sensitize attention or constrain it; late consultation is not proof of induction. Log assumptions and the researcher's relation to the setting when they materially affect interpretation, without treating a reflexivity statement as validity certification.

## Check wording and support separately

Use [quote-check.md](quote-check.md) only for local literal fidelity. Record the matching manifestation/version and confirm that it is the one being cited; an exact match in an older version does not verify wording in a newer one. A passage can match exactly and still be selective, sarcastic, contradicted nearby or unable to support the proposed theme. Record contextual review separately, including those failures. Do not repair a failed candidate quotation into an apparently verified one; retain the failed candidate and make any correction a distinct proposal. Human/model thematic overlap or consensus supplies no independent interpretive gold standard.

## Return

Deliver an analytic memo or candidate structure suited to the question: linked supplied expressions, clearly marked proposals, rivals/negative cases, relationship warrants, decision state and unresolved questions. Include a bounded statement of what was examined and why work stopped. Use [limits.md](limits.md) if the request asks for saturation, historical comparison, transfer, performance or general validity.
