# What the expanded sample changed

The pilot had six interviews. Two adaptive waves added sixteen, for 22 bound interviews. All 344 originals were lexically screened and hash-checked; 322 were not qualitatively analyzed in this study. Selection follows conceptual uncertainty, not representativeness or a target count. Multiple accounts from one interview are not independent cases. See the exact before-coding records in C002_SELECTION.json and C003_SELECTION.json.

“2.0,” “4.0” and “V2” are archive filenames, not verified event dates. The two Shipper interviews share a creator; Elena/Lazar describe connected Lovable practices; vendor and organizational ties are not independent replications. The Amol personal medical segment is intentionally outside the analysis.

| Pilot proposition or gap | Added evidence | Consequence for the current theory |
|---|---|---|
| Review/revision routes may preserve direction | Support product shut down after repeated fixes; Proof produced rewrites without verified durable recovery | A review channel and actual recoverability are separate |
| Experienced users may have more capacity to correct | Reported noncoder achievements, expert exhaustion, specialist use of AI, and external API/model changes | Disaggregate manual coding, domain knowledge, AI practice, time and infrastructure |
| Human takeover might preserve agency | Documentation-based experiment with unknown result; reported agent-selected tool switch | Keep several possible intervention routes; no empirical necessity claim |
| Acceptable output may indicate effective delegation | Investor email matched intended content but skipped expected approval | Satisfaction, authorization and effective intervention must be distinguished |
| More capability may increase workload | Reported coordination rule, extra software demand, mediated workload concerns | Investigate how capacity becomes new commitments and who sets them |
| Overload or diminished voice might be widespread | Senior leader’s refusal, public leadership challenge, personally enjoyable use | Retain contrary cases; neither general coercion nor general freedom established |
| More feasible ideas may widen agency | Prototypes lead to early stopping; privacy revision reportedly impairs performance | Investigate whether people preserve purposes when declining or constraining capability |
| AI may cause organizational change | Explicit reorganization attribution; broad turnaround combining cuts, strategy, values and AI | Keep organizational structure and business pressure as rival explanations |

The five review findings narrowed claims rather than removing inconvenient cases. They preserved expected approval in the email record, replaced broad novice language, made organizational causality conditional, left the manual-fallback question unresolved, and acknowledged expertise/support in Sander’s local prompt improvement. See audit/DISPOSITIONS.md.

This run stops with unresolved discriminators, not saturation. The most informative next evidence is a direct worker disagreement or refusal followed through a known organizational response; durable outcomes after repair; and comparable tasks with resources and decision rights recorded before success or failure. The archive screens did not establish that such material is absent everywhere. REQ003 preserves the gap without substituting additional founder narratives for it.
