# Federal Register inventory

The included inventories cover 806,941 document records from January 2000 to 15 September 2026. Identity is publication date plus document number; this is not a count of agencies, rules or regulatory events. There are 806,237 records with text and 704 without text.

All 321 monthly TSV inventories are supplied in 27 annual archives under ../data/inventories/. The monthly index ../data/MANIFEST_INDEX.tsv gives counts, sizes and SHA-256 fingerprints; ../data/COVERAGE.json gives coverage totals. The webpage’s year/month explorer is a view of these records.

An inventory row records the document identity, text status, source and file fingerprint. Missing text remains part of the sampling frame. Agency and title are in document headers rather than separate inventory fields. Images in served text may be placeholders; counts of missing images are lower bounds.

Historical text packs are not supplied. The two-day example includes email-redacted text and a working replay. The original listing/download programs were not retained, so the historical acquisition cannot be replayed in full. Source websites can subsequently correct or add documents; a current retrieval may differ from the snapshot.
