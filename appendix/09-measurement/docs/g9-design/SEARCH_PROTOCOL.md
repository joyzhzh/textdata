# Search guidance for future measurement work

The catalogue is a research design; these searches have not been run. Choose an explicit date boundary and retain the publication version and inspection depth for each source. Search relevant languages and report actual coverage limits.

## Eligibility

Include a work if it defines or operationalizes a relevant novelty construct, validates or challenges such a measure, specifies the human criterion, or supplies a concrete method component or domain-transfer bridge. Include null results, replications, corrections and adverse comparisons. Human and non-LLM methods are required baselines even though LLM assisted measurement is the focal question.

Generation-only papers enter only when their evaluation contributes a relevant measure or validation question. General anomaly detection, optimization novelty search, authorship detection, plagiarism screening and recommender serendipity enter only with an explicit measurement bridge. A model called “novel,” a high citation count, venture funding, sales, patents granted or an innovation award is not sufficient eligibility as a novelty measure. Those outcomes may be extracted as imperfect criteria or consequences with the exact interpretation retained.

Search broadly for concepts before applying narrow exclusions. “Atypicality,” “distinctiveness,” “originality,” “newness,” “uncommonness,” “contribution,” “recombination,” “surprise” and “new information” are related search terms, not synonyms to collapse in synthesis.

## Starting queries

| Family | Example starting query |
|---|---|
| Ventures | `(venture OR entrepreneurial opportunity OR business model) (novelty OR newness OR originality) (measure OR validation)` |
| Crowd ideas | `(crowdsourcing OR idea contest OR user innovation) (novelty OR uniqueness) (reference set OR expert rating OR language model)` |
| Culture | `(film OR story OR music OR visual art) (novelty OR originality OR atypicality) (content OR structure OR style OR measure)` |
| Science | `(scientific idea OR hypothesis OR research contribution) (novelty assessment OR originality) (prior literature OR expert OR retrieval)` |
| Engineering | `(engineering design OR functional principle OR ideation) (novelty OR variety) (metric OR validity)` |
| Organizations | `(organizational practice OR process innovation OR employee idea) (newness OR novelty) (scale OR text OR assessment)` |
| Education | `(divergent thinking OR student creativity OR alternate uses) (originality OR semantic distance) (validity OR automated scoring)` |
| Public and social | `(policy innovation OR social innovation OR institutional design) (novelty OR new to) (measurement OR evaluation)` |
| LLM roles | `(large language model OR LLM) (novelty OR originality) (judge OR retrieval OR extraction OR calibration)` |
| Adverse | `(method or domain name) (bias OR validity OR failure OR contamination OR disagreement)` |

Use disciplinary sources, earlier methods, citation links and adverse evidence. Verify original publication identities, versions, supplements and corrections. Keep source access and licences distinct from whether a method is scientifically supported. A search result is not full-text inspection.

## Interpretation

Distinguish a work from its preprint, conference and journal versions, and track shared data or labels when judging independent corroboration. Absence from a bounded search is not evidence that a method does not exist. Separate first appearance, audience-relative newness, artifact properties and descriptions. The unassessed application–method matrix is a starting map, not a literature census.
