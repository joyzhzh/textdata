Five passages were selected for illustration. The assistant that built the prototype authored the candidates interactively. They have not been independently validated.

u1 — Section 1, paragraph 2
Source opening: “Agencies are already leading the way in the use of AI by applying it”.
Candidate: no_assignment. The passage describes uses; it does not direct an action. The export preserves a no-assignment row rather than dropping the passage. This is an interpretation of the passage, not confirmation that its described uses occurred.

u2 — Section 4(b), first sentence
Actor: “the Director of OMB (Director)”.
Action: “publicly post a roadmap for the policy guidance that OMB intends to create or revise to better support the use of AI”.
Candidate modality: mandatory.
Preserved qualifiers: within 180 days of the order; coordination with identified stakeholders; consistency with the order. Stakeholders remain in a separate role field. They are not assigned the Director's posting action.

u3 — Section 5(b)
Actor: “each agency”.
Action: “prepare an inventory of its non-classified and non-sensitive use cases of AI”.
Candidate modality: mandatory.
Preserved qualifiers: deadline tied to the CIO Council directive; annual repetition; section 9 scope; current and planned uses; consistency with the agency mission. The Council's mention is a temporal trigger, not another responsible actor for preparation.

u4 — Section 5(c)(ii)
Actor: “agencies” at the second occurrence in the selected passage.
Action: “strive to implement the approved plans”.
Candidate modality: mandatory, applied to the exact action to strive.
Preserved qualifiers: coordination participants; 180 days after plan approval; “subject to existing resource levels”. This record does not strengthen the wording to a requirement to achieve implementation. The source quote begins at normalized character 10636 for the acting “agencies”; exported offsets, rather than this prose, govern exact location.

u5 — Section 5(e)
Actor: “agencies”.
Action: “make their inventories available to the public”.
Candidate modality: mandatory.
Preserved qualifiers: 120 days after inventories are completed; practicability; applicable-law/policy and protected-information limitations. The public is a recipient. Actual publication is not established.
