# Survey method and limits

The survey organizes existing documentary assessments into **22,667 typed entries across 14 research functions**. The public index contains **22,640 entries**; 27 are excluded for privacy. These are research items and evidence units, not a count of unique software systems or independent studies.

Identity grouping uses explicit work identifiers, DOI identities, or a matching normalized name together with a matching primary source locator. A shared name alone does not join records. Some duplicates and relationships remain unresolved; sharing a source address does not establish identical works or independent corroboration.

Each assessment considers eight questions: purpose; task and setting; mechanism; inputs and outputs; interfaces and dependencies; reported evaluation and conditions; limitations and contrary evidence; and version, date and access. The index records which questions remain unresolved, not the complete source text. A gap can reflect missing evidence or incomplete extraction; it does not prove that a capability is absent.

Function and setting labels support navigation. They are not independently validated classifications. Proposed TextData uses are design interpretations, not measured benefits or recommendations to adopt.

The source assessments differ in dates, access and search scope. The survey does not establish worldwide completeness or independently validate every primary work. No surveyed software was installed or run. Reported benchmark results remain tied to their tasks and conditions.

The index omits 19,401 summary passages without a clear privacy or reuse basis. It retains 15 checked project notes, 3,167 description-gap notices and 57 unresolved-assessment notices. These distinctions describe available text, not the quality of a system.
