# Methods evidence informing the Grounded Theory toolkit

The engine’s methods inquiry informed the [Grounded Theory toolkit](../../05-grounded-theory/README.md), whose [methods basis](../../05-grounded-theory/tool/references/methods-basis.md) connects source evidence to its operations.

The resulting workflow requires analysts to identify source lines for incidents, state what a comparison changes, record responses to negative cases, and keep further sampling requests proposed for the researcher to decide. The methods basis distinguishes source-supported choices from single-source proposals, inherited defaults and analyst adaptations.

The inquiry admitted 44 current evidence records. One source recheck corrected an AI reviewer’s claim: the paper’s author-contributions section does identify all four evaluators as coauthors. The toolkit retains the corrected qualification. This is a documented correction, not an estimate of model error rates.

The synthesis remains provisional. Review was unblinded, model identities were self-reported, and no independent second search tested completeness. The underlying source and review workspace is private. Software checks and the toolkit’s empirical application are described with component 5; they do not establish the validity of every methodological choice.
